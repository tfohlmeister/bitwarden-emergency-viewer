Vault Viewer

Opens vault-export.json with your master password. Everything happens in this browser window; nothing is sent anywhere.

Choose a file or drop it here vault-export.json from the unpacked archive

Click any value to copy it. Passwords stay hidden until you reveal them. Close the window when you are done and the decrypted contents are gone with it. On a machine that is not yours, the clipboard is the one leftover you have to clear yourself.
